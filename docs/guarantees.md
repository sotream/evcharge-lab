# Distributed-systems guarantees

What each guarantee rests on, and the drill that tested it.

- [ADR 0012](adr/0012-kafka-replication-and-producer-backpressure.md): Kafka replication, producer durability and gateway backpressure. Drills: [`kafka-broker-loss.sh`](../drills/kafka-broker-loss.sh) with one and two brokers down.
- [ADR 0015](adr/0015-read-replica-and-read-your-writes-for-sessions.md): the sessions read replica and read-your-writes by LSN. Drill: [`replica-lag.sh`](../drills/replica-lag.sh), run `replica-lag-20261108T183638Z` (11 of 11 checks): the replica was held back for 240 s, peak lag 239.4 s and drained 9 s after release; 0 read-your-writes violations in 624 checks; reads of a driver's own writes fell back to the primary after the 200 ms wait; driver API read p99 0.173 s during the pause (target < 0.3 s). Two earlier runs are kept with notes.
- [ADR 0016](adr/0016-consensus-kraft-quorum.md): the KRaft controller quorum, quorum maths, and what losing the quorum does. Drill: [`kraft-quorum-loss.sh`](../drills/kraft-quorum-loss.sh), run `kraft-quorum-loss-20261108T185039Z` (12 of 12 checks) with 2 of 3 nodes stopped for about 165 s (18:51:44Z to 18:54:29Z, `events.log`): 200 of 200 stations stayed connected, 0 RemoteStopTransaction sent, quorum back in 4 s, first acknowledged produce after 8 s, 0 of 393 probe readings missing (178 generated during this run, 117 while the nodes were down; the rest are left over from run T184440Z). Targets missed during the outage: OCPP CALLRESULT p99 reached 2.48 s (target < 0.5 s) and telemetry ingest lag p99 29.9 s during and 59.2 s after (target < 5 s).
- [ADR 0017](adr/0017-consistency-models.md): the consistency model, mechanism and staleness bound of every piece of state.
- [ADR 0018](adr/0018-postgres-failover-and-backup-restore.md): Postgres failover and backup/restore, async against sync (Apple M4 Pro, one run per row). Drills: [`postgres-failover.sh`](../drills/postgres-failover.sh), [`backup-restore.sh`](../drills/backup-restore.sh). The replica promotes and the services reconnect in 0.37 to 0.48 s (DB RTO), but drivers see 1.1 to 10.3 s consistent with the driver-api circuit breaker (openMs 10 s) opening; the breaker state was not recorded. **RPO 0 is not met with the asynchronous replica:** when the replica was cut off for 3 s before the primary was killed, 282 and 281 acknowledged writes were lost in two runs (probe commits over 3.1 s); the rerun also lost 10 acknowledged reservations and left 32 overlapping pairs of acknowledgements, each with a lost reservation on one side. With a synchronous standby the same fault lost 0, at the price of a 3.64 s write gap. Backup: 163,044 KiB (159 MiB) in 1.07 s, restored into a separate container in 2.10 s, all 10 checks passed (readiness plus 9 integrity checks), data age 52.7 s (no WAL archiving, so nothing after the backup comes back).
- [ADR 0019](adr/0019-invariant-checking-under-faults.md): the reservation invariant checked from an operation history ([`load/history/`](../load/history/), unit-tested) under a sessions pause (15 s), a sessions restart and a primary failover. Passed under the pause and the restart; failed (10 lost acknowledged reservations, 32 overlapping pairs each with a lost reservation on one side) in the async failover with an unreachable replica; with the constraint switched off the checker reports 292 violations in 883 operations. It is not a linearizability check; the ADR says what it does not prove.
- Consumer group rebalance ([ADR 0012](adr/0012-kafka-replication-and-producer-backpressure.md), [`consumer-rebalance.sh`](../drills/consumer-rebalance.sh)): a killed member is dropped after 45.1 and 45.3 s (two runs), the survivor owns all 24 partitions after 46.2 and 46.4 s, a restarted member is back in 0.2 to 0.4 s, the lag peak is 371 and 409 records, 0 readings lost. Offsets moved back by 200 records per partition: 4,800 records were read again and all 4,800 were suppressed by the unique index (0 duplicate rows).
- Load from another machine: [`load/run-remote.sh`](../load/run-remote.sh) (see the runbook). Not run yet; remote numbers will be kept apart from local ones.
- 24 h station offline replay at simulator time scale 60 (`station-offline-replay-20261108T190213Z`, 20 stations offline for 1,440 simulated minutes): replay queues empty 40 s after the after phase began (about 49 s after the offline window ended; the reconnect time itself is not recorded), 123,652 probe readings generated, 0 missing, 0 duplicate rows. The replayed-row flag is not checked at time scale 60. The earlier INCONCLUSIVE run (151006Z) is kept.
- Other drills in [`drills/`](../drills/): gateway instance loss, reconnect storm, slow Postgres, smart-charging down. Raw output is in `docs/drill-output/`.
